# Brand-Adaptive Opportunity Indicators

## Rule

Select only evidence-supported categories that help the prospect understand the brand’s strengths. Do not force fixed categories. Omit categories with insufficient evidence. Keep launch risks in a separate section.

## Possible categories

Choose 5–8 where relevant:

- Brand-story clarity
- Hero-product visibility
- Product-line coherence
- Premium positioning consistency
- Evidence-led communication
- Visual merchandising strength
- Giftability
- Repeat-purchase structure
- Localization flexibility
- Launch-test flexibility
- International-commerce foundation
- Retail-partner readiness
- Sustainability proof points
- Community or content strength
- Product education quality

## Never score without evidence

Do not create scores for demand, sales potential, market size, competitor success, regulatory approval, or consumer acceptance unless validated by reliable evidence.
