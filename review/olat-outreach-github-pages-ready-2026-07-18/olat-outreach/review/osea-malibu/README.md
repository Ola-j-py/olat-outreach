## Olat Studio scoring rule

Opportunity indicators are adapted to each brand. Use 5–8 categories supported by public evidence. Do not force every brand into the same scoring categories. If evidence is weak or absent, omit the category. Do not reduce the opportunity score merely because launch work remains; pricing, regulation, labeling, fulfillment, and channel questions belong in the validation and risk sections.

Scores describe observable brand assets and validation potential. They do not predict Japanese demand, sales, market share, or regulatory approval.
