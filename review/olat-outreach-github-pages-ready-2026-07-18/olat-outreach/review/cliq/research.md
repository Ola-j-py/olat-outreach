# CLIQ — Internal Research Notes

Research date: 2026-07-17  
Purpose: Japan market-entry outreach  
Public snapshot rule: source names only; URLs remain internal.

## Fact / Evidence / Assessment / Recommendation

### 1. Current sales access

**Fact**  
CLIQ’s official FAQ says international shipping is unavailable and the company currently ships only within the United States.

**Evidence**  
- CLIQ FAQs: https://www.cliqproducts.com/pages/faqs
- CLIQ Shipping, Returns and Warranty Policy: https://www.cliqproducts.com/pages/shipping-returns-and-warranty-policy

**Assessment**  
Based on the available evidence, Japanese customers cannot currently complete a normal direct purchase through CLIQ’s official U.S. store. This is a market-access gap, not evidence of demand.

**Recommendation**  
Validate a controlled Japan route before investing in a full launch: importer/reseller, limited DTC test, or a small wholesale pilot.

### 2. Product proposition

**Fact**  
The current ClassiQ product page lists a price of US$149.95, a folded size of 3.4 × 3.4 × 13.5 inches, weight of 3.53 lb, and capacity of 400 lb.

**Evidence**  
- CLIQ ClassiQ product page: https://www.cliqproducts.com/products/cliq-chair
- CLIQ product collection/comparison: https://www.cliqproducts.com/collections/all

**Assessment**  
Based on the available evidence, portability is the strongest Japan-relevant product attribute. Japan relevance has not been validated by sales data.

**Recommendation**  
Test messaging around compact storage, quick setup, travel, event use, and emergency preparedness rather than relying only on “camping chair” positioning.

### 3. Product range and positioning

**Fact**  
CLIQ publicly lists ClassiQ, MystiQ, EpiQ, and Riviera models, with current listed prices ranging from US$149.95 to US$349.

**Evidence**  
- CLIQ product collection/comparison: https://www.cliqproducts.com/collections/all
- CLIQ homepage: https://www.cliqproducts.com/

**Assessment**  
Based on the available evidence, the range occupies a premium price position before Japan freight, duty, tax, localization, and support costs.

**Recommendation**  
Model landed retail prices and compare them with products actually available in Japan before choosing the launch SKU.

### 4. External product evidence

**Fact**  
Independent editorial coverage has highlighted the ClassiQ’s compact folded size and portability.

**Evidence**  
- Food & Wine review: https://www.foodandwine.com/cliq-portable-camping-chair-amazon-7495156
- SFGATE camping-chair guide: https://www.sfgate.com/shopping/article/best-camping-chairs-20221948.php
- Real Simple lawn-chair guide: https://www.realsimple.com/home-organizing/decorating/outdoor-living/best-lawn-chairs

**Assessment**  
This supports the credibility of the portability proposition. It does not establish Japanese demand, willingness to pay, or channel fit.

**Recommendation**  
Use portability as the primary hypothesis in Japanese customer interviews, landing-page tests, and retailer outreach.

### 5. B2B route

**Fact**  
CLIQ has a public bulk-order program and directs sales inquiries to sales@cliqproducts.com. The page mentions retail opportunities and bulk orders.

**Evidence**  
- CLIQ Custom/Bulk Orders: https://www.cliqproducts.com/pages/custom-chair-orders

**Assessment**  
Based on the available evidence, the sales team is the most relevant initial contact for discussing a Japan test or retail route.

**Recommendation**  
Send the snapshot to sales@cliqproducts.com and propose a fixed-scope validation report before a launch project.
