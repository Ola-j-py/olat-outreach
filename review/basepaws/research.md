# Basepaws — Research Notes

Prepared for Olat Studio outreach. Updated: 2026-07-18.

## Fact

- Basepaws says it was founded in 2017 and created the first at-home comprehensive cat DNA test and oral health test for cats.
- Basepaws became part of Zoetis in 2022.
- The Breed + Health Cat DNA Test combines breed analysis, genetic health markers, and trait information.
- Basepaws offers a Cat Oral Health Test focused on active signs associated with dental disease.
- The workflow uses at-home sample collection, online kit registration, laboratory processing, and a digital report.
- International customers are responsible for return postage to the Basepaws laboratory in the United States.
- The official Basepaws contact page lists paws@basepaws.com for influencers and partners.
- Yano Research Institute estimated Japan's FY2024 pet-related market at ¥1.9108 trillion and forecast ¥1.9257 trillion for FY2025.
- Japan's National Veterinary Assay Laboratory explains that veterinary medicinal products, veterinary medical devices, and veterinary in-vitro diagnostics can require applications or notifications under Japan's veterinary product framework.

## Assessment

- The clearest first Japan proposition is not a broad pet launch. It is a focused premium cat-health pilot.
- The Breed + Health Cat DNA Test is the strongest hero product because it combines emotional discovery with practical health information.
- Japanese education and report guidance are central to conversion because the product requires explanation and trust.
- Veterinary, feline-specialist, breeder, and premium pet-health partners are promising early validation channels.
- International sample-return logistics are a core part of the customer experience and should be designed before broader promotion.
- Product claims and the exact Japanese regulatory pathway should be reviewed with an appropriate veterinary regulatory specialist before commercial launch.

## Recommendation

1. Build a Japan offer around the Breed + Health Cat DNA Test.
2. Define one priority customer segment and one partner segment.
3. Create Japanese education, sample instructions, and report-navigation content.
4. Model landed pricing including outbound shipping, return postage, tax, and partner margin.
5. Run a small partner-led pilot and collect structured feedback.
6. Use the Oral Health Test as a second-stage preventive-care extension.
