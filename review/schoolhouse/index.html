Recommended Validation

A three-stage route from product edit to pilot launch.

01

Curate

Select a visually coherent 6–10 SKU hardware edit with clear finish, dimension, and use-case logic.

02

Validate

Test landed pricing, installation fit, product storytelling, and partner interest with a small group of Japan-based professionals.

03

Pilot

Launch through one focused partner or project, collect evidence, and use the findings to decide on assortment expansion and lighting feasibility.

Launch Considerations

Four workstreams that strengthen the launch plan.

Landed pricing

Freight, duties, tax, and partner margin must preserve a credible premium position.

Dimensions and installation

Hardware sizes, mounting details, and installation guidance should be checked against local project needs.

Partner route

The first partner should be selected for brand fit, specification capability, and quality of feedback—not only reach.

Lighting feasibility

Selected fixtures require a dedicated review of electrical standards, importer responsibilities, labeling, and installation compatibility.
