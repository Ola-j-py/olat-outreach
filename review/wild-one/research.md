# Wild One — Research Notes

Prepared: 2026-07-18

## Fact

- Wild One describes its mission as making life with dogs and cats look as good as it feels, with design-forward, function-first products.
- Current categories include dog walk products, walk kits, travel, treats, grooming, home, toys, and a cat collection.
- Wild One sells cushioned harnesses, step-in harnesses, waterproof collars, multiple leash formats, poop bag carriers, and coordinated walk kits.
- Wild One offers international shipping to locations accessible via UPS.
- Wild One provides a wholesale route and directs wholesale inquiries to salesteam@verygreat.nyc.
- Japan's Pet Food Safety Act applies to pet food businesses, and Japanese labeling requirements apply to pet food sold in Japan.

## Evidence

- The About page emphasizes design, function, simplicity, and support for animal rescue.
- Product and collection pages show coordinated colors, multiple sizes, functional comparisons, and bundled Walk Kits.
- The FAQ and Shipping Policy confirm international shipping and identify the wholesale contact.
- MAFF materials identify requirements for pet-food business operators and required Japanese labeling fields.

## Assessment

- Wild One's strongest first Japan concept is a focused Walk Capsule rather than a broad all-category launch.
- A coordinated 8–12 SKU edit can communicate the brand clearly and support bundle purchasing.
- Harnesses, collars, leashes, and matching accessories provide a lower-complexity first phase than treats or other consumables.
- Clear Japanese size guidance and landed-pricing validation will be central to conversion and retail acceptance.
- Cat, travel, home, and play products provide credible expansion paths after the walk assortment is validated.

## Recommendation

1. Select 8–12 hero products across harnesses, collars, leashes, and matching accessories.
2. Limit the first color palette and size range to create a coherent pilot.
3. Test landed pricing and retail margin with specialty pet, lifestyle, and curated e-commerce partners.
4. Localize size guidance, fit comparisons, care instructions, and functional product benefits.
5. Treat cat, travel, home, and play as phase-two categories.
6. Review treats separately under Japan's Pet Food Safety Act before commercial launch.
